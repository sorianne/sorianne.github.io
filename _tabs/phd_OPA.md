# Fundamental concept

## effective aperture

   effective aperture相当于光栅阵列的发射面积，决定了OPA的divergence angle
   
   <img width="786" height="202" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4fa3b13f-818c-4d6e-8195-a310aea4e53e" />

  长度 $$l$$: 由单个光栅的**发射长度**来决定
  
  宽度 $$w$$: 由**channel数量$$N$$**和**光栅间距$$d$$**决定
